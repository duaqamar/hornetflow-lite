# HornetFlow financial aid appeal policy — SYNTHETIC DEMO

This workshop policy is not Sacramento State policy. All student records are fictional.

## Required information
An appeal packet needs a matched student ID, full name and term; an explanation of circumstances; how academic progress was affected; and a specific academic recovery plan.

## Supporting documents
For the Illness category, a supporting document must be marked available. Other categories in this simplified demo have no additional required attachments. Availability is self-reported; the prototype does not upload, inspect or authenticate documents.

## Review and outcome
The student reviews and confirms their statement before downloading a complete packet. Missing items must be displayed. Completeness does not establish eligibility for aid or guarantee approval. The student must consult their university's actual policies and submit through its official process. The app never submits an appeal.

## Personal statements
Use only circumstances the student provides. Do not invent diagnoses, dates, grades, expenses or outcomes. A student may edit the statement at any time. Changes require review again.
